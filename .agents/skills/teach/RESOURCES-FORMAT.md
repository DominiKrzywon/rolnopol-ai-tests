# Resources format

Use this for the teaching workspace's `RESOURCES.md`:

```md
# {Topic} Resources

## Knowledge

- [{Source title and author or organization}]({Verified source URL})
  Covers: {Relevant material}. Use for: {Specific lesson or question}.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [{Community name}]({Verified community URL})
  Use for: {Practical feedback it can provide and why it is credible}.

## Gaps

- {Missing evidence or a topic without an adequate source}
```

Omit empty optional sections. Prefer primary sources, recognized experts, and
peer-reviewed work. Annotate every entry and verify its actual content.
Replace incorrect, shallow, or irrelevant sources rather than accumulating them.
Record any preference against community participation so it is respected later.
Knowledge comes from sources; communities can add contextual practitioner judgment.
See the [teaching workflow](SKILL.md) for their roles.
