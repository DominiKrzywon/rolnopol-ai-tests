# Glossary format

Use this for the teaching workspace's `GLOSSARY.md`:

```md
# {Topic} Glossary

{One or two sentences defining this glossary's scope.}

## Terms

**{Canonical term}**:
{One or two sentences defining what the concept is.}
_Aliases or wording to avoid_: {Only when needed to resolve ambiguity}
```

Add a term only once the user demonstrates understanding, not merely after its
introduction. Prefer precise canonical terminology throughout lessons, records,
and other definitions. Explain aliases rather than banning established synonyms
without a reason. State how an ambiguous term is used in this workspace.
Group terms when useful and revise definitions as understanding deepens.
